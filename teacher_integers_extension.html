ROOM 01 · 3 PUZZLES

The Temperature Chamber

Three damaged systems contain multi-step integer clues. Solve each before the chamber unlocks.

01.1🌡️ Arctic Temperature Log
At 06:00 a research station is at −18°C. It warms by 11°C, drops by 23°C, then warms by 16°C. What is the final temperature?
Answer: -14
Hint

Track each change in order: start −18, then +11, −23, +16.

Worked example

Example: −12 + 9 − 14 + 6 = −11°C.

Mini-lesson

A rise is addition and a fall is subtraction. You may group positive and negative changes: 11 + 16 = 27 and −18 − 23 = −41; the total is −14.

01.2💻 Missing Sensor Reading
Four temperature readings have a mean of −6°C. Three readings are −15°C, 8°C and −11°C. What is the missing fourth reading?
Answer: -6
Hint

A mean of −6 over four readings means their total is −24.

Worked example

Example: Three values average −2, so total −6. If two are −5 and 4, the missing value is −5.

Mini-lesson

Mean = total ÷ number of readings. Here the total is 4 × (−6) = −24. Known readings sum to −18, so the missing value is −24 − (−18) = −6.

01.3🔢 Elevation Relay
A drone starts 45 m above sea level, descends 72 m, rises 19 m, then descends another 36 m. What is its final signed elevation in metres?
Answer: -44
Hint

Represent the changes as 45 − 72 + 19 − 36.

Worked example

Example: 30 − 50 + 12 = −8 m.

Mini-lesson

Use zero for sea level. A positive elevation is above sea level; negative is below. 45 − 72 = −27, then +19 = −8, then −36 = −44.

ROOM 02 · 3 PUZZLES

The Order of Operations Lab

Three harder BEDMAS expressions each yield a positive integer. Enter their answers in A–B–C order.

02.1🧪 Reactor A
Evaluate 48 ÷ (−6) + 5 × (−3 + 9) − 7
Answer: 15
Hint

Evaluate −3 + 9 first, then division and multiplication.

Worked example

Example: 24 ÷ (−4) + 3 × (−2 + 7) = −6 + 15 = 9.

Mini-lesson

Brackets first: −3 + 9 = 6. Then 48 ÷ (−6) = −8 and 5 × 6 = 30. Finally −8 + 30 − 7 = 15.

02.2⚙️ Reactor B
Evaluate (−7) × (−4) − 36 ÷ (−3) − 19
Answer: 21
Hint

Calculate the product and quotient before subtracting.

Worked example

Example: (−5) × (−3) − 20 ÷ (−4) − 6 = 15 − (−5) − 6 = 14.

Mini-lesson

(−7) × (−4) = 28; 36 ÷ (−3) = −12. So 28 − (−12) − 19 = 21.

02.3📡 Reactor C
Evaluate 6 × [18 ÷ (−3) + 11] − 8
Answer: 22
Hint

Inside the square brackets, divide before adding.

Worked example

Example: 4 × [12 ÷ (−3) + 7] − 5 = 4 × 3 − 5 = 7.

Mini-lesson

18 ÷ (−3) = −6; −6 + 11 = 5; 6 × 5 − 8 = 22.

ROOM 03 · 4 PUZZLES

The Comparison Observatory

Compare the VALUES of the expressions, not just the numbers you see. Use < or > for each station and the four-symbol door lock.

03.1🔭 Deep Freeze
Compare −3 × (8 − 13) ___ −18 + 4. Choose < or >.
Answer: >
Hint

Evaluate both sides: a negative times a negative is positive.

Worked example

Example: −2 × (5 − 9) > −6 + 3 because 8 > −3.

Mini-lesson

Left: −3 × (−5) = 15. Right: −18 + 4 = −14. Therefore left > right.

03.2🪐 Orbit Monitor
Compare −48 ÷ 6 + 5 ___ −2 × (−4). Choose < or >.
Answer: <
Hint

Calculate each side separately.

Worked example

Example: −20 ÷ 5 + 1 < −3 × (−2), since −3 < 6.

Mini-lesson

Left: −8 + 5 = −3. Right: 8. Therefore −3 < 8.

03.3🧭 Pressure Gauge
Compare −(−17 + 9) ___ 3 × (−2) + 1. Choose < or >.
Answer: >
Hint

The negative sign outside brackets reverses the sign of the bracket value.

Worked example

Example: −(−8 + 3) > −2 × 2, since 5 > −4.

Mini-lesson

Left: −(−8) = 8. Right: −6 + 1 = −5. Thus 8 > −5.

03.4🌙 Lunar Scanner
Compare (−6) × (−5) − 41 ___ −72 ÷ (−8) − 4. Choose < or >.
Answer: <
Hint

The left expression is negative; the right is positive.

Worked example

Example: (−3)(−4) − 20 < −30 ÷ (−5), since −8 < 6.

Mini-lesson

Left: 30 − 41 = −11. Right: 9 − 4 = 5. So left < right.

ROOM 04 · 4 PUZZLES

The Power Generator

Four generator circuits combine integer multiplication and division with real-world reasoning. Enter the four signed answers.

04.1⚡ Circuit A — Descent
A lift descends 7 floors per minute for 6 minutes, then rises 19 floors. If it starts at floor 12, which floor does it finish on?
Answer: -11
Hint

Use 12 + (−7 × 6) + 19.

Worked example

Example: Starting at 5, descending 4 floors/min for 3 min and rising 2 gives 5 − 12 + 2 = −5.

Mini-lesson

A descent of 7 floors per minute for 6 minutes is −42 floors. Starting at 12: 12 − 42 + 19 = −11.

04.2🔋 Circuit B — Energy Debt
A machine loses 9 energy units in each of 8 cycles, then gains 120 units shared equally across 6 cells. What is the net change for one cell if the loss is also shared equally across those 6 cells? (Enter the net change per cell.)
Answer: 8
Hint

Total net change is −9 × 8 + 120 = 48. Divide by 6.

Worked example

Example: Total loss −30 and gain 50 across 4 cells gives (−30 + 50) ÷ 4 = 5 each.

Mini-lesson

−9 × 8 = −72; −72 + 120 = 48. Across six cells, 48 ÷ 6 = 8.

04.3🌀 Circuit C — Reverse Signal
A signal is multiplied by −6 and then increased by 15 to give −57. What was the original integer signal?
Answer: 12
Hint

Undo the +15 first, then divide by −6.

Worked example

Example: −4x + 5 = −15 gives −4x = −20, so x = 5.

Mini-lesson

Let the starting signal be x. −6x + 15 = −57. Subtract 15: −6x = −72. Divide by −6: x = 12.

04.4🔌 Circuit D — Repeated Loss
A research fund loses $18 per day for 9 days, then receives two equal grants of $95. What is the overall change in dollars?
Answer: 28
Hint

Combine the nine losses and two gains.

Worked example

Example: −12 × 4 + 2 × 30 = 12.

Mini-lesson

Nine losses total −18 × 9 = −162. Two grants total 190. Net change is −162 + 190 = 28.

ROOM 05 · 4 PUZZLES

The Security Vault

These vault clues require careful interpretation, reverse reasoning and multi-step integer calculations.

05.1🗝️ Vault A — Missing Balance
An account starts at −$85. After three identical deposits and a $34 withdrawal, the balance is $49. How much is EACH deposit?
Answer: 56
Hint

The three deposits together must overcome −85 and −34 to reach 49.

Worked example

Example: −20 + 2d − 10 = 30 means 2d = 60 and d = 30.

Mini-lesson

Write −85 + 3d − 34 = 49. Then 3d − 119 = 49, so 3d = 168 and d = 56.

05.2📦 Vault B — Submarine
A submarine begins at −145 m, rises 38 m, dives 3 times as far as it rose, then rises 67 m. What is its final depth as a signed number of metres?
Answer: -154
Hint

The dive is −3 × 38 = −114 m.

Worked example

Example: −80 + 20 − 2 × 20 + 15 = −85 m.

Mini-lesson

−145 + 38 − 114 + 67 = −154 m. Interpret a dive as a negative change.

05.3🧲 Vault C — Unknown Integer
Three consecutive integers have a sum of −72. What is the smallest of the three integers?
Answer: -25
Hint

The middle integer equals the average of the three.

Worked example

Example: Three consecutive integers sum to 18: 5, 6, 7. Smallest is 5.

Mini-lesson

The middle integer is −72 ÷ 3 = −24. So the integers are −25, −24, −23.

05.4🧰 Vault D — Rival Teams
Team A starts with −18 points and earns 7 points in each of 8 rounds. Team B starts with 35 points and loses 4 points in each of 6 rounds. By how many points does Team A finish ahead of Team B?
Answer: 27
Hint

Find each final score, then subtract B from A.

Worked example

Example: A = −5 + 3 × 6 = 13; B = 20 − 2 × 5 = 10; A leads by 3.

Mini-lesson

Team A: −18 + 7 × 8 = 38. Team B: 35 − 4 × 6 = 11. Difference: 38 − 11 = 27.

ROOM 06 · 6 PUZZLES

The Direction Decoder

Interpret the SIGN of each mathematical situation. A negative final value and a negative change are not the same thing.

06.1📈 Signal A — Cooling Rate
The temperature changes from −14°C to −3°C. Is the CHANGE positive (+) or negative (−)?
Answer: +
Hint

Compare the final temperature with the initial temperature.

Worked example

Example: −10°C to −6°C is an increase of +4°C.

Mini-lesson

Change = final − initial = −3 − (−14) = +11°C. It is a positive change despite both temperatures being negative.

06.2🧭 Signal B — Displacement
East is positive. A robot travels 12 m west, 20 m east, then 15 m west. Is its NET displacement positive or negative?
Answer: -
Hint

Represent west as negative and east as positive.

Worked example

Example: −5 + 9 − 8 = −4, a negative displacement.

Mini-lesson

−12 + 20 − 15 = −7 m. The net displacement is west, so negative.

06.3🏦 Signal C — Debt
A bank balance changes from −$120 to −$45. Is the CHANGE positive or negative?
Answer: +
Hint

The balance is still negative, but has it improved?

Worked example

Example: −50 to −20 is a +30 change.

Mini-lesson

−45 − (−120) = +75. A less-negative debt represents an increase in the balance.

06.4🌊 Signal D — Depth
Sea level is 0 m. A submarine moves from −30 m to −82 m. Is its CHANGE in elevation positive or negative?
Answer: -
Hint

Moving deeper means a lower signed elevation.

Worked example

Example: −10 to −25 changes by −15.

Mini-lesson

−82 − (−30) = −52 m. The change is negative.

06.5📉 Signal E — Net Profit
A business makes $70, loses $120, then makes $40. Is the NET result positive or negative?
Answer: -
Hint

Add the three signed amounts.

Worked example

Example: +30 − 50 + 10 = −10.

Mini-lesson

70 − 120 + 40 = −10. The net result is a loss, so negative.

06.6🛗 Signal F — Recovery
An elevator is at floor −8, descends 5 floors, then rises 17 floors. Is its FINAL floor number positive or negative?
Answer: +
Hint

Calculate the final floor: −8 − 5 + 17.

Worked example

Example: −4 − 3 + 10 = +3.

Mini-lesson

−8 − 5 + 17 = +4. The final position is positive.

ROOM 07 · 4 PUZZLES

The Final Escape

Insert ONE pair of brackets in each equation to make it true. Negative integers and BEDMAS matter. Solve all four to escape.

07.1🧩 Bracket Gate A
Insert one pair of brackets into −8 + 5 × −3 to make 9.
Answer: (−8 + 5) × −3 = 9
Hint

Group the first two integers before multiplying by −3.

Worked example

Example: (−6 + 2) × (−4) = 16.

Mini-lesson

(−8 + 5) × (−3) = (−3) × (−3) = 9.

07.2🧪 Bracket Gate B
Insert one pair of brackets into −36 ÷ −9 + 5 to make 9.
Answer: −36 ÷ (−9 + 5) = 9
Hint

Make the divisor negative by adding −9 and 5.

Worked example

Example: −24 ÷ (−8 + 2) = 4.

Mini-lesson

−36 ÷ (−9 + 5) = −36 ÷ (−4) = 9.

07.3🛰️ Bracket Gate C
Insert one pair of brackets into −7 − 11 × −4 to make 72.
Answer: (−7 − 11) × −4 = 72
Hint

Calculate −7 − 11 first.

Worked example

Example: (−5 − 3) × (−2) = 16.

Mini-lesson

(−7 − 11) × (−4) = −18 × (−4) = 72.

07.4🚀 Bracket Gate D
Insert one pair of brackets into 48 ÷ −6 − −2 to make -12.
Answer: 48 ÷ (−6 − −2) = -12
Hint

Group the last two numbers to make a negative divisor.

Worked example

Example: 30 ÷ (−7 − 3) = −3.

Mini-lesson

48 ÷ (−6 − (−2)) = 48 ÷ (−4) = −12.
