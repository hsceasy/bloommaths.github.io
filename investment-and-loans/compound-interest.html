Compound interest

Investment and Loans course notes

Key concept

Interest is added to the balance each period.

A = P(1+r)ⁿ

Worked example 1

Invest $1000 at 5% annually for 2 years.

1000(1.05)²=$1102.50.

Worked example 2

Find interest earned on $2000 at 10% for 2 years.

2000(1.1)²−2000=$420.

Common mistake

Match rate and period.
