Maximum flow and minimum cut

Network Flow course notes

Key concept

A cut separates source from sink. Its capacity sums forward edges crossing the cut.

Maximum flow = minimum cut capacity

Worked example 1

A series path has capacities 8 and 5. Find maximum flow.

5, the bottleneck.

Worked example 2

Two independent parallel paths have capacities 4 and 6. Find maximum flow.

4+6=10.

Common mistake

Do not sum all edges in a series path.
