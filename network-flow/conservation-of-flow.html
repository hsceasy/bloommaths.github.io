Conservation of flow

Network Flow course notes

Key concept

At an intermediate junction, incoming flow equals outgoing flow.

Total inflow = total outflow

Worked example 1

A junction receives 4 and 7 units; one outgoing edge carries 6.

Other outgoing flow=11−6=5.

Worked example 2

A junction receives 9 and sends 3 and 6. Is flow conserved?

Yes, 9=3+6.

Common mistake

Source and sink are exceptions.
