Viorosa Maths · Year 11 · Mathematics Standard

Nonlinear formulas

Key idea

Isolate the power before taking a root. In measurement, choose the nonnegative root.

Worked example 1 — square roots

Question: A circular garden has area 49π m². Find its radius.

Solution

A = πr²

49π = πr²

49ππ = πr²π

49 = r²

√49 = r (r ≥ 0)

r = 7 m

Worked example 2 — cube roots

Question: A cube has volume 125 cm³. Find its side length.

Solution

V = s³

125 = s³

∛125 = ∛(s³)

5 = s

s = 5 cm

Common mistake: Forgetting the square root.
