Minimum spanning trees

Key idea

Connect all vertices with minimum total edge weight and no cycles. Kruskal selects cheapest available edges that do not form cycles.

Worked example 1

Triangle weights 2,3,7 → select 2 and 3; total 5.

Worked example 2

AB = 1, BC = 2, AC = 3, CD = 4 → select AB, BC, CD; total 7.

Common mistake: Confusing a shortest route with a spanning tree.
