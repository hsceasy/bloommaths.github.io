Weighted networks and shortest paths

Key idea

Add edge weights along a route and compare totals, not the number of edges.

Worked example 1

AB = 4, BC = 3, AC = 9: A–B–C costs 7, shorter than AC.

Worked example 2

AB = 2, BD = 8, AC = 4, CD = 3: A–C–D costs 7 versus 10.

Common mistake: Choosing the fewest edges automatically.
