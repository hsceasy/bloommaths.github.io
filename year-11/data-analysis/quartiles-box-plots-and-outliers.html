Quartiles box plots and outliers

Key idea

IQR = Q3 − Q1. Potential outliers lie below Q1 − 1.5IQR or above Q3 + 1.5IQR.

Worked example 1

Q1 = 10, Q3 = 18 → IQR 8, fences −2 and 30.

Worked example 2

With these fences, 35 is a potential outlier; 30 is not.

Common mistake: Confusing range and IQR.
