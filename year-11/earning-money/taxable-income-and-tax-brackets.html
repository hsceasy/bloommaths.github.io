Viorosa Maths · Year 11 Mathematics Standard

Taxable income and tax brackets

Key idea

Subtract allowable deductions from assessable income. Apply marginal rates only to the portion in each bracket.

Worked example 1

$60,000 income less $1500 deductions → $58,500 taxable income.

Worked example 2

Illustrative tax: first $20,000 tax-free, then 20%; $35,000 → $3000 tax.

Common mistake: Applying the top marginal rate to all income.
