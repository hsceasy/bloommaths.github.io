Wages and overtime

Key idea

Multiply hours by the applicable hourly rate, then add the separate earnings.

Worked example 1

38 h at $25 plus 4 h at time-and-a-half → 950 + 150 = $1100.

Worked example 2

6 h at $30 and 2 h at double time → 180 + 120 = $300.

Common mistake: Applying overtime to all hours.
