Viorosa Maths · Year 11 Mathematics Standard

Bias and survey design

Key idea

Use neutral questions and a representative sampling frame. Consider nonresponse and voluntary response bias.

Worked example 1

Survey only gym members about exercise: likely coverage bias.

Worked example 2

Replace “How great is our library?” with “How satisfied are you with our library?”

Common mistake: Assuming a large biased sample is representative.
