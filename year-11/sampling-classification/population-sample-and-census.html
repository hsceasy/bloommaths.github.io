Viorosa Maths · Year 11 Mathematics Standard

Population sample and census

Key idea

A population is the full group of interest; a sample is a subset; a census attempts to include every member.

Worked example 1

Survey 80 of 800 students: sample 80, population 800.

Worked example 2

Ask all 25 class members: census of that class.

Common mistake: Generalising beyond the target population.
