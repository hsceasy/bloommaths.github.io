UTC offsets and daylight saving

Key idea

Subtract the origin UTC offset, then add the destination offset. Use offsets supplied for the date.

Worked example 1

15:00 at UTC+10 → 05:00 UTC → 00:00 at UTC−5.

Worked example 2

08:00 at UTC+11 → 07:00 at UTC+10.

Common mistake: Adding both offsets without considering signs.
