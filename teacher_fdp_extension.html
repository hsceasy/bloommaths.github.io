📋 Classroom setup

  • Before class: Open the student game on student devices and check that it loads. Students need a browser and somewhere to record their working.
  • Lesson launch: Explain that students must solve four puzzles per room to unlock the next area. Encourage showing calculations, not just entering answers.
  • During play: Circulate, ask students to justify strategies and use the room topics below to target support.
  • After play: Ask students to reflect on one conversion or calculation they found difficult and explain their method.

Suggested use: Individual work, pairs or small groups. Timing will vary with prior knowledge and support; you can split the 12 rooms across lessons.

This hub is a reference and printable answer key. It does not collect student results, and the answer key is not password protected. Share the student game link rather than this page with students.

🎯 Learning focus

Practise equivalent and simplified fractions; comparing fractions, decimals and percentages; mixed and improper fractions; decimal place value and rounding; operations with fractions and decimals; percentages of quantities; and percentage change in everyday contexts.

Topic descriptions reflect the game content. Check your local syllabus outcomes and differentiate as appropriate for your class.

ROOM 01 · 4 QUESTIONS

Maths Classroom

Equivalent fractions & simplifying · Extension problem solving

01.1The maths teacher has 96 revision cards. 38 are fraction cards and 14 are decimal cards. The rest are percentage cards. How many percentage cards are there?
Answer: 36
Teaching hint

Add the two fractions, subtract from 1, then multiply by 96.

01.2A workbook contains 180 questions. Maya completes 25 on Monday and 13 of the whole workbook on Tuesday. How many questions remain?
Answer: 48
Teaching hint

Find the fraction remaining: 1 − 2/5 − 1/3.

01.3The teacher cuts 56 metre of ribbon into pieces each 112 metre long. She uses 4 pieces on a display. How many pieces are left?
Answer: 6
Teaching hint

Find the total number of pieces first, then subtract 4.

01.4Of 120 students, 35 bring a calculator. Of those students, 23 also bring a ruler. How many students bring both?
Answer: 48
Teaching hint

Multiply 120 by 3/5, then multiply the result by 2/3.

ROOM 02 · 4 QUESTIONS

Science Laboratory

Compare & order FDP · Extension problem solving

02.1A scientist prepares 240 mL of solution. 38 is blue dye and 25% is yellow dye; the rest is water. How many millilitres of water are used?
Answer: 90
Teaching hint

Convert 25% to 1/4, then find the remaining fraction of 240.

02.2Under a microscope, 35% of 200 cells are stained. Of the unstained cells, 25 are damaged. How many unstained cells are NOT damaged?
Answer: 78
Teaching hint

First find 65% of 200, then keep 3/5 of that.

02.3Three flasks contain 0.75 L, 58 L and 40% of a 2 L bottle. How many litres do the three flasks contain altogether?
Answer: 2.175
Teaching hint

Convert all amounts to litres before adding.

02.4A 1.5 L mixture is 20% concentrate. After adding 0.5 L of water, what percentage of the NEW mixture is concentrate?
Answer: 15
Teaching hint

Find 20% of 1.5 L, then divide by the new 2 L total and multiply by 100.

ROOM 03 · 4 QUESTIONS

History Classroom

Mixed & improper fractions · Extension problem solving

03.1A history display uses 212 metres of red fabric and 134 metres of blue fabric. The curator buys 5 metres altogether. How much fabric remains, in metres?
Answer: 34
Teaching hint

Add the mixed numbers, then subtract from 5.

03.2A museum owns 84 replica coins. 27 are Roman and 13 are Greek. The rest are Egyptian. How many Egyptian coins are there?
Answer: 32
Teaching hint

Subtract both fractions from 1 and multiply by 84.

03.3A timeline is 335 metres long. Each century occupies 310 metre. How many centuries can the full timeline show?
Answer: 12
Teaching hint

Convert 3 3/5 to an improper fraction, then divide by 3/10.

03.4A restoration team completes 114 panels on Monday and 223 panels on Tuesday. Their target is 5 panels. How many panels remain, as a simplified fraction?
Answer: 1312
Teaching hint

Subtract 1 1/4 and 2 2/3 from 5; an improper fraction is fine.

ROOM 04 · 4 QUESTIONS

Geography Classroom

Decimal place value & rounding · Extension problem solving

04.1On a map, 1 cm represents 2.5 km. Two routes measure 3.6 cm and 4.85 cm. How many kilometres longer is the second route?
Answer: 3.125
Teaching hint

Subtract the map lengths, then multiply by 2.5.

04.2A weather station records 18.745°C, 19.365°C and 20.125°C. Find the mean temperature and round it to TWO decimal places.
Answer: 19.41
Teaching hint

Add the three readings and divide by 3. Round only at the end.

04.3A school trip travels 18.75 km, then 7.68 km, and returns 4.95 km along the same route. How many kilometres from the start is the bus?
Answer: 21.48
Teaching hint

Add the outward distances and subtract the distance back.

04.4A hiking route is 12.8 km. The class walks 37.5% before lunch and then another 2.35 km. How many kilometres remain?
Answer: 5.65
Teaching hint

Find 37.5% of 12.8, add 2.35, and subtract from 12.8.

ROOM 05 · 4 QUESTIONS

Gymnasium

Adding & subtracting fractions · Extension problem solving

05.1In a relay, Ava runs 38 km, Ben runs 512 km, and Cora runs 16 km. How many kilometres do they run altogether?
Answer: 2324
Teaching hint

Use a common denominator of 24.

05.2A team trains for 212 hours. Warm-up takes 25 hour and drills take 34 hour. How many hours remain for the match?
Answer: 2720
Teaching hint

Convert to twentieths, then subtract from 2 1/2.

05.3A runner completes 56 of a 1.2 km circuit, then runs another 0.35 km. How many kilometres has the runner covered?
Answer: 1.35
Teaching hint

Find 5/6 of 1.2 km, then add 0.35 km.

05.4The sports cupboard has 72 balls. 13 are basketballs. Of the remaining balls, 38 are volleyballs. How many balls are neither?
Answer: 30
Teaching hint

Remove the basketballs, then find 5/8 of the remainder.

ROOM 06 · 4 QUESTIONS

Library

Decimal forms & representations · Extension problem solving

06.1A library receives 320 new books. 38 are fiction and 22.5% are biographies. How many books are in other categories?
Answer: 128
Teaching hint

Find both groups, then subtract from 320.

06.2A student reads 0.375 of a 240-page book on Monday and 14 on Tuesday. How many pages remain unread?
Answer: 90
Teaching hint

Find the total fraction read, then multiply the remaining fraction by 240.

06.3The library has a $480 budget. It spends 35% on novels, then 14 of the ORIGINAL budget on reference books. How much money remains?
Answer: 192
Teaching hint

Both percentages are of the original $480.

06.4Of 180 borrowed books, 29 are returned late. A quarter of those late books have damaged covers. How many books are both late and damaged?
Answer: 10
Teaching hint

Find 2/9 of 180, then 1/4 of that result.

ROOM 07 · 4 QUESTIONS

Music Room

Multiplying & dividing fractions · Extension problem solving

07.1A song lasts 312 minutes. The introduction is 27 of the song, and the chorus is 35 of the remaining time. How many minutes are left for verses?
Answer: 1
Teaching hint

Find the introduction, then take 3/5 of what remains for the chorus.

07.2A drum pattern has 48 beats. 38 are accented, and 23 of the accented beats are played loudly. How many beats are loud accents?
Answer: 12
Teaching hint

Find 3/8 of 48, then 2/3 of that.

07.3The orchestra rehearses for 2.4 hours. Strings receive 37.5% of the time and brass receive 14. How many MINUTES are left for woodwinds?
Answer: 54
Teaching hint

Subtract both fractions from the whole, then convert hours to minutes.

07.4A violinist practices 56 hour on each of 4 days. She spends 25 of her total practice time on scales. How many hours does she spend on scales?
Answer: 43
Teaching hint

Multiply 5/6 by 4, then by 2/5.

ROOM 08 · 4 QUESTIONS

Computing Lab

Decimal operations · Extension problem solving

08.1A spreadsheet tracks 24.6 GB of data. After compressing it by 35%, the school downloads another 3.15 GB. How many GB are stored now?
Answer: 19.14
Teaching hint

Keep 65% of 24.6, then add 3.15.

08.2A program runs 1.25 million calculations per second for 0.8 seconds, then another 0.35 million calculations. How many MILLION calculations in total?
Answer: 1.35
Teaching hint

Multiply the rate by the time, then add 0.35.

08.3A computer has 16 GB of memory. The operating system uses 2.75 GB, and four programs each use 1.625 GB. How many GB remain?
Answer: 6.75
Teaching hint

Subtract 2.75 and four lots of 1.625 from 16.

08.4A download is 7.2 GB. The first 2.4 GB arrives in 0.8 minutes. At the same constant rate, how many MORE minutes are needed?
Answer: 1.6
Teaching hint

Find the download rate, then divide the remaining size by it.

ROOM 09 · 4 QUESTIONS

Cafeteria

Fractions & percentages of amounts · Extension problem solving

09.1The cafeteria makes 160 sandwiches. 38 are vegetarian. It sells 75% of the vegetarian sandwiches and 60% of the others. How many sandwiches are UNSOLD?
Answer: 55
Teaching hint

Find both types, calculate how many of each sell, then subtract from 160.

09.2A recipe makes 24 muffins using 0.75 kg of flour. The cook must make 40 muffins but has already used 0.2 kg from a 1.5 kg bag. How many kilograms of flour will remain after baking?
Answer: 0.05
Teaching hint

Scale 0.75 kg by 40/24, then subtract that from 1.3 kg.

09.3A lunch costs $12.80. It is discounted by 15%, then a $1.50 drink is added. What is the final cost in dollars?
Answer: 12.38
Teaching hint

Find 85% of 12.80, then add 1.50.

09.4A dispenser contains 18 L of juice. Students drink 29 before recess and 25% of the ORIGINAL amount after recess. How many litres remain?
Answer: 9.5
Teaching hint

Both amounts are fractions of the original 18 L.

ROOM 10 · 4 QUESTIONS

English Classroom

FDP conversions & reasoning · Extension problem solving

10.1The class anthology has 240 pages. Poetry occupies 310 and stories occupy 35%. The rest is artwork. How many pages are artwork?
Answer: 84
Teaching hint

Subtract 30% and 35% from the whole, then multiply by 240.

10.2A student writes 1,200 words. She deletes 15%, then adds 25% of the remaining word count. How many words are in the final draft?
Answer: 1275
Teaching hint

Find 85% of 1,200, then increase that result by 25%.

10.3A book club reads 58 of a novel in week one and 20% of the WHOLE novel in week two. If 84 pages remain, how many pages are in the novel?
Answer: 480
Teaching hint

Find the fraction remaining, then divide 84 by it.

10.4A journal has 96 entries. 14 are poems. Of the remaining entries, 23 are short stories. How many entries are neither poems nor stories?
Answer: 24
Teaching hint

Remove poems, then find 1/3 of the remaining entries.

ROOM 11 · 4 QUESTIONS

Main Office

Percentage change & money · Extension problem solving

11.1The office orders 24 folders at $3.75 each. A 20% bulk discount applies to the folders, then a $12 delivery fee is added. What is the total bill?
Answer: 84
Teaching hint

Find the full folder cost, apply the discount, then add delivery.

11.2The fundraiser collects $1,250. It spends 18% on supplies and 25 of the ORIGINAL amount on equipment. How much money remains?
Answer: 525
Teaching hint

Both expenses are based on the original total.

11.3A printer costs $480 before GST. The supplier gives a 15% discount, then adds 10% GST to the discounted price. What is the final price?
Answer: 448.8
Teaching hint

Multiply by 0.85, then by 1.10.

11.4The office buys 80 notebooks for $2.40 each and sells them for $3.60 each. If 65 notebooks sell, what is the total profit after accounting for the cost of ALL 80?
Answer: 42
Teaching hint

Revenue is 65 × 3.60; total cost is 80 × 2.40.

ROOM 12 · 4 QUESTIONS

Principal’s Office

Mixed FDP problem solving · Extension problem solving

12.1The principal allocates $3,600 for excursions. Transport uses 35% of the budget, and venue fees use 512 of the ORIGINAL budget. How much money remains?
Answer: 840
Teaching hint

Subtract both allocations from the original budget.

12.2At assembly, 78 of 320 students attend. Of those attending, 15% receive an award. How many attending students do NOT receive an award?
Answer: 238
Teaching hint

Find attendance first, then 85% of the attending students.

12.3A filing cabinet contains 240 records. 30% are archived. Of the remaining records, 37 need updating. How many records are current and do not need updating?
Answer: 96
Teaching hint

Find the 70% not archived, then 4/7 of those.

12.4The final security code is the original cost of a device. After a 20% discount and then 10% GST on the discounted price, the device costs $264. What was its ORIGINAL price?
Answer: 300
Teaching hint

The final price is 0.8 × 1.1 = 0.88 of the original.
