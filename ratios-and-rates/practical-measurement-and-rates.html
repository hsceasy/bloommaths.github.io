Key concept

Use consistent units for area, rainfall, energy and flow.

Trapezoid area = ½(a+b)h

Worked example 1

Parallel sides 4 m and 8 m, height 3 m.

½×12×3=18 m².

Worked example 2

A 2 kW heater runs for 3 hours. Find energy.

2×3=6 kWh.

Common mistake

Power and energy are different quantities.
