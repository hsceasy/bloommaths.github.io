Normal probability and expected frequency

The Normal Distribution course notes

Key concept

Use the area under the curve as probability.

Expected count = probability × population

Worked example 1

About 95% of 200 observations lie within two SDs. Estimate count.

0.95×200=190.

Worked example 2

A cumulative table gives P(Z≤1)=0.8413. Find P(Z>1).

1−0.8413=0.1587.

Common mistake

Check which area the table gives.
