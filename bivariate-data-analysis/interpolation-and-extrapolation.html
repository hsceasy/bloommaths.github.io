Interpolation and extrapolation

Bivariate Data Analysis course notes

Key concept

Interpolation predicts within the observed x range; extrapolation predicts outside it.

Check observed minimum and maximum x.

Worked example 1

Data cover ages 10–16. Predict at age 12: which method?

Interpolation.

Worked example 2

Predict at age 25: which method?

Extrapolation, usually less reliable.

Common mistake

Do not assume a trend continues indefinitely.
