Future value of an annuity

Annuities course notes

Key concept

An ordinary annuity has equal payments at the end of each period.

FV = R[(1+r)ⁿ−1]/r

Worked example 1

Deposit $100 at each year end for 2 years at 5%.

100×1.05+100=$205.

Worked example 2

Deposit $200 at each year end for 3 years at 10%.

200(1.21+1.1+1)=$662.

Common mistake

The last payment earns no interest yet.
