Pearson correlation coefficient

Bivariate Data Analysis course notes

Key concept

Pearson’s r measures strength and direction of linear association.

−1 ≤ r ≤ 1

Worked example 1

Interpret r=−0.92.

Strong negative linear association.

Worked example 2

Interpret r=0.

No linear association; a nonlinear relationship may exist.

Common mistake

Do not interpret r as a slope.
