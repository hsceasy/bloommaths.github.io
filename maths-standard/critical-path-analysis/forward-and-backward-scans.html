Forward and backward scans

Critical Path Analysis course notes

Key concept

Forward scans take the maximum incoming finish; backward scans take the minimum outgoing latest start.

EF = ES + duration; LS = LF − duration

Worked example 1

Two incoming activities finish at 5 and 8. Find earliest event time.

max(5,8)=8.

Worked example 2

Two outgoing activities must start by 12 and 15. Find latest event time.

min(12,15)=12.

Common mistake

Use maximum forward and minimum backward.
