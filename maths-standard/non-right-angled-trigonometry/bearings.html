Bearings

Non-right-angled Trigonometry course notes

Key concept

A true bearing is measured clockwise from north using three digits.

Reverse bearing = bearing ± 180° (within 0°–360°)

Worked example 1

A boat travels on bearing 065°. Find the return bearing.

065°+180°=245°.

Worked example 2

Two paths from one point have bearings 040° and 110°. Find the included angle.

110°−40°=70°.

Common mistake

Measure from north, not east.
