Trapezoidal rule

Key idea

For equally spaced ordinates, multiply spacing by half the sum of the end ordinates plus the interior ordinates.

Worked example 1

Widths 3, 5, 4 m at 2 m spacing → area ≈ 17 m².

Worked example 2

Widths 2, 6 m separated by 4 m → area ≈ 16 m².

Common mistake: Counting interior ordinates only once inside the doubled sum.
