Viorosa Maths · Year 11 Mathematics Standard

Perimeter area and composite shapes

Key idea

Split composite shapes into familiar nonoverlapping parts, or subtract missing regions.

Worked example 1

Rectangle 8 by 5 m minus 2 by 3 m cutout → 34 m².

Worked example 2

Triangle base 12 cm height 7 cm → 42 cm².

Common mistake: Using a sloping side as perpendicular height.
