Viorosa Maths · Year 11 Mathematics Standard

Practical measurement and costing

Key idea

Calculate the required quantity, include stated waste and round whole purchases up.

Worked example 1

12 m² at $25 per m² → $300.

Worked example 2

Need 23 m² with 10% waste → 25.3 m²; 2 m² packs → 13 packs.

Common mistake: Rounding down the number of packs.
