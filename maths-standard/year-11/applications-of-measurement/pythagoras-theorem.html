Pythagoras theorem

Key idea

For right triangles, c² = a² + b², with c opposite the right angle.

Worked example 1

Legs 6 and 8 → c = 10.

Worked example 2

Hypotenuse 13, leg 5 → other leg = 12.

Common mistake: Adding squares when finding a shorter side.
