Volume capacity and surface area

Key idea

Volume measures space inside; surface area measures the outside. 1 L = 1000 cm³.

Worked example 1

Box 10 × 20 × 30 cm → 6000 cm³ = 6 L.

Worked example 2

Closed cube side 4 cm → surface area 96 cm².

Common mistake: Confusing square and cubic units.
