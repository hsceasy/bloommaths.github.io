Viorosa Maths · Year 11 Mathematics Standard

Circles arcs and sectors

Key idea

Use the fraction θ360 of the circumference or circle area for a sector.

Worked example 1

Radius 6 cm, angle 60°: arc = 2π cm.

Worked example 2

Radius 4 cm, angle 90°: sector area = 4π cm².

Common mistake: Using diameter instead of radius.
