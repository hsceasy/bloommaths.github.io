Gradient and intercepts

Key idea

In y = mx + b, m is the change in y per unit of x and b is the value at x = 0.

Worked example 1

y = 2x + 3 has gradient 2 and intercept (0,3).

Worked example 2

y = −x + 3 has gradient −1 and intercept (0,3).

Common mistake: Confusing gradient with intercept.
