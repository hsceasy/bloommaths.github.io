Viorosa Maths · Year 11 Mathematics Standard

Linear models and intersections

Key idea

Represent a fixed cost by the intercept and a variable cost by the gradient. Equal costs occur at an intersection.

Worked example 1

C = 12 + 3n; n = 8 → C = $36.

Worked example 2

10 + 4n = 22 + 2n → n = 6; both cost $34.

Common mistake: Ignoring the fixed charge.
