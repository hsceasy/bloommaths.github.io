Direct variation

Key idea

Direct variation has form y = kx and passes through the origin.

Worked example 1

y = 18 when x = 6 → k = 3; y = 3x.

Worked example 2

y = 4x; x = 7 → y = 28.

Common mistake: Adding a nonzero intercept.
