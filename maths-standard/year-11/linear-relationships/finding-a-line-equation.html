Viorosa Maths · Year 11 Mathematics Standard

Finding a line equation

Key idea

Find gradient from rise divided by run, then substitute a point to find b.

Worked example 1

(1,4), (3,8): m = (8−4)(3−1) = 2; b = 2; y = 2x + 2.

Worked example 2

(0,5), (2,1): m = −2; y = −2x + 5.

Common mistake: Reversing only one coordinate difference.
