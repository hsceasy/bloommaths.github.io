Viorosa Maths · Year 11 Mathematics Standard

Algebraic fractions

Key idea

Multiply every term by the lowest common denominator.

Worked example 1

x3 + 2 = 6 → x + 6 = 18 → x = 12.

Worked example 2

x4 + x2 = 9 → x + 2x = 36 → x = 12.

Common mistake: Multiplying only the fraction terms.
