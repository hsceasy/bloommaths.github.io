Viorosa Maths · Year 11 Mathematics Standard

Substitution and formulas

Key idea

Replace each variable with its given value, using brackets for negatives.

Worked example 1

A = lw; l = 8, w = 3 → A = 24.

Worked example 2

v = u + at; u = 4, a = 2, t = 5 → v = 14.

Common mistake: Ignoring order of operations.
