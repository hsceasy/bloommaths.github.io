Viorosa Maths · Year 11 Mathematics Standard

GST

Key idea

For a taxable item at 10% GST, multiply the pre-tax price by 1.10. Divide the inclusive price by 1.10 to remove GST.

Worked example 1

$90 before GST → $99.

Worked example 2

$220 inclusive → $200 before GST; GST $20.

Common mistake: Subtracting 10% of the inclusive price.
