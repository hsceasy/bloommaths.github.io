Latitude and longitude

Key idea

Latitude measures north or south of the equator; longitude measures east or west of Greenwich. Earth rotates about 15° per hour.

Worked example 1

30° longitude difference corresponds to 2 h of ideal solar time.

Worked example 2

20°N to 15°S gives 35° latitude difference.

Common mistake: Assuming legal time zones follow longitude exactly.
