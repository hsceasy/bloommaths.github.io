Viorosa Maths · Year 11 Mathematics Standard

Elapsed time and timetables

Key idea

Convert to a common clock format and account for crossing midnight.

Worked example 1

09:45 to 12:10 = 2 h 25 min.

Worked example 2

22:40 to 01:15 next day = 2 h 35 min.

Common mistake: Treating minutes as decimal hundredths.
