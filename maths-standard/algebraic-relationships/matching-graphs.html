Recognising linear, quadratic, exponential and reciprocal graphs

Algebraic Relationships course notes

Key concept

Identify the graph family using its shape, intercepts, turning points and asymptotes.

Linear: straight line · Quadratic: parabola · Exponential: growth or decay · Reciprocal: two branches

Worked example 1

Which model has a constant multiplicative factor and approaches zero in one direction?

An exponential model with positive starting value and base not equal to 1.

Worked example 2

Which model has two branches separated by x = 0 and no intercepts?

The reciprocal model y = k/x, k ≠ 0.

Common mistake

A curved graph is not necessarily exponential.
