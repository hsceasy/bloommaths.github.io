Capture recapture

Ratios and Rates course notes

Key concept

Estimate population from two samples under stable population and equal capture assumptions.

N ≈ marked first × second samplemarked recaptures

Worked example 1

Mark 40, later catch 50 including 10 marked.

40×5010=200.

Worked example 2

Mark 30, catch 60 including 15 marked.

30×6015=120.

Common mistake

Zero recaptures cannot be used in this formula.
